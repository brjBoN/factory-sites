Our Story · Est. 1998

Three crazy people
wanted a bakery.

“We are Three Crazy Bakers because there once were three crazy people who wanted a bakery.”

Larry and Donna Grimm and their daughter Paige opened the bakery in 1998 on the corner of South Main Street and First Avenue — and it has been there ever since. What started with cinnamon rolls and pies grew into a full-service restaurant serving lunch and dinner seven days a week.

Today, owners Maggie and Hart Brown carry the tradition forward with the same scratch-made baking that started it all. Come to Moultrie and eat some of the best food East of the Mississippi.
