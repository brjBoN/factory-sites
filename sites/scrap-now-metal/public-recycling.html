Good to know

Asked at every yard.

Is there a minimum load size?
Whether it's a trunk full of cans or a trailer of tin, call first and the team will tell you if it's worth the trip and what it's paying today.
Can I bring appliances or old equipment?
Appliances, farm implements, and equipment are commonly recycled — but some items have special handling rules. Describe what you've got when you call and you'll know before you load it.
What should I bring with me?
Scrap yards in Georgia typically require a photo ID for metal purchases. Ask when you call so you arrive with exactly what's needed — no wasted trips.
