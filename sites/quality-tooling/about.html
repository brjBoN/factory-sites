— Our story. —

A small, family-owned machine shop in the small southern town of Moultrie, Georgia — in business since 1985.

Est. 1985

Forty years of holding tolerance.

Quality Tooling started as a full-service machine shop — custom machining, CNC milling, laser cutting, welding, and powder coating for farms and industry across South Georgia. Four decades later, that’s still the backbone of what we do.

The metal art studio grew out of the same discipline. When you’ve spent your life machining parts to a thousandth of an inch, a monogram isn’t decoration — it’s a part, and parts get made right. Every piece is laser-cut and powder-coated in-house so it stands the test of time.

“No cookie-cutter here — unless you want one ;)”

What we makeBoth sides of the shop

Industrial parts and repairs · monograms & wall decor · patina letters · wedding décor · grave markers & memorials · mailbox toppers · business signage · patent-pending fishing rod holders.
