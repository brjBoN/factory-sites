drainage expertise —

A better drainage plan starts with the whole property.

Standing water is only the symptom. The real work is understanding where runoff starts, how grade and soil influence it, and where the water can safely go.

ECS brings the earthwork, drainage, culvert, and site-preparation capabilities together so the solution is considered as one working system—not a patch placed on top of the problem.

  • Drain tile, French drains, basins, and solid pipe

how ECS solves drainage problems —

Collection, conveyance, grading, and discharge—planned together.

The right combination depends on the grade, soil, runoff, and available discharge point.

01

Surface water collection

Grading, swales, inlets, catch basins, and trench drains collect stormwater before it settles where it should not.

02

Underground conveyance

French drains, drain tile, solid pipe, and connected basins create a dependable route away from problem areas.

03

Site grading & protection

Culverts, erosion control, site grading, and thoughtful discharge points help the solution work with the property around it.
