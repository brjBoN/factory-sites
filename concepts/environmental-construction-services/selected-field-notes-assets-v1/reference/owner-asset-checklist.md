# Owner Asset and Content Checklist

Confirm these items before any official implementation or launch:

- [ ] Authoritative vector or higher-resolution logo master and production-use
      permission.
- [ ] Current service priorities and whether every listed category remains
      offered.
- [ ] Exact cities, counties, service radius, and travel policy.
- [ ] Current business hours and after-hours or emergency policy.
- [ ] Preferred phone, email, address, and inquiry workflow.
- [ ] Owner-approved company story beyond `family-owned and operated`.
- [ ] Supported licenses, insurance, bonding, certifications, affiliations,
      awards, warranties, financing, and estimate language, if any.
- [ ] Approved descriptions of equipment, methods, materials, capacities,
      project sizes, timelines, and staffing.
- [ ] Owner-controlled project photographs with written web-use permission and
      accurate captions.
- [ ] Privacy, intake, storage, retention, and response policy before adding any
      live form.
- [ ] Accessibility contact method and final accessibility statement.
- [ ] Written authorization for public deployment, DNS changes, analytics,
      indexing, and removal of the private-concept notice.

Until those items are confirmed, keep the preview private, unindexed, and
non-collecting.
