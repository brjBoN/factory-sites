## Generated illustration masters

Three OpenAI built-in ImageGen calls created:

- the six-service atlas;
- the desktop excavation/culvert hero; and
- the portrait mobile excavation/culvert hero.

The exact prompts are in `PROMPTS.md`. The selected concept was a style
reference only. The new generated masters contain no generated ECS logo.

All construction, terrain, water, equipment, and project-like imagery is
illustrative. It must never be described as an ECS employee, asset, property,
method, project, result, or technical record.
